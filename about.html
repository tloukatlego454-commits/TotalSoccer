About TotalSoccer

Discover our story, mission, and commitment to the beautiful game.

Our History

TotalSoccer was founded in 2026 by Katlego Tlou, a passionate soccer player and entrepreneur who identified a gap in the market for high-quality online soccer retail. What started as a small shop in Vaal has grown into a trusted e-commerce destination serving customers across South Africa.

Today, TotalSoccer is recognized as a leading brand known for its extensive selection of authentic products, exceptional customer service, and genuine passion for the beautiful game.
